`prepare-data.mjs` requires a local `data/problems.raw.json` with at least ~100 entries before it will run. `extract-tuf-links.mjs` expects an offline copy of the A2Z page saved at `/private/tmp/tuf-a2z.html` prior to execution. Batch verification runs via `node scripts/verify-batch.mjs batch1 batch2 ...` and merging via `node scripts/merge-batch.mjs batch1 --write` (dry-run without `--write`).
